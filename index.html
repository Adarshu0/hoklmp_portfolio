Designing with intention. Engineering with discipline.

I am Adarsh Kumar Singh, currently pursuing my B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering at JECRC University, Jaipur. Simultaneously, I work as an independent digital designer and frontend developer obsessed with micro-interactions, typography, and fluid user experiences.

My foundation in electronics gives me a unique mental model: I understand systems from the silicon level — signals, logic gates, and microcontroller registers — all the way up to semantic DOM structures, CSS architectures, and visual design systems in Figma.

I believe modern digital products shouldn't feel like generic templates. Great work requires generous negative space, intentional typography, frictionless user flows, and code architectures that run at a smooth 60 frames per second.
